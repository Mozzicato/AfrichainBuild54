# Africhain Demo Video Pitch Script

## Target

Record a confident 2 minute 30 second to 3 minute demo video.

The goal is to prove three things:

1. Africhain is working.
2. Voice changes real business data.
3. The product can grow into a market intelligence and finance layer for informal traders.

---

## Before Recording

1. Open Chrome.
2. Run `npm run dev`.
3. Open `http://localhost:3000`.
4. Click `Reset` so the demo starts from clean seeded data.
5. Keep these pages ready:
   - `/`
   - `/business`
   - `/market`
   - `/receipts`
6. If voice recognition mishears you, use the typed input and say: "Markets are noisy, so Africhain also supports typed fallback."

---

## 0:00 - 0:15: Opening

**Show:** Home page.

**Say:**

"This is Africhain, a voice-first operating system for Africa's informal traders. Instead of forcing a trader to fill forms, Africhain lets them speak naturally and turns that speech into real business records."

---

## 0:15 - 0:35: Show The Three Voice Modes

**Show:** Click `Log sales`, `Ask live prices`, and `Update inventory`.

**Say:**

"We split the voice experience into three modes so there is no confusion. Sales mode handles money and debts. Prices mode handles AMIN market intelligence. Inventory mode handles stock updates."

---

## 0:35 - 0:55: Record A Sale

**Action:** Select `Log sales`.

Say or type:

`I sell six paint of rice for nine thousand naira`

**Say:**

"Africhain understands the item, quantity, unit, and amount. It records the sale, updates today's revenue, and reduces rice from inventory."

---

## 0:55 - 1:15: Record Customer Debt

Say or type:

`Mama Nkechi collect two bag of sugar, she never pay, na five thousand`

**Say:**

"It also understands credit sales. Mama Nkechi is now added to the debt list, so the trader does not lose track of money owed."

Optional quick command:

`Who dey owe me?`

---

## 1:15 - 1:40: Update Inventory

**Action:** Select `Update inventory`.

Say or type:

`I buy ten bag of sugar, add am to stock`

Then open `/business`.

**Say:**

"Inventory is not a separate spreadsheet. It updates as the trader speaks. The dashboard shows stock quantity, unit, low-stock threshold, and restock status."

---

## 1:40 - 2:05: Show AMIN Live Prices

**Action:** Go back home and select `Ask live prices`.

Say or type:

`How much yam dey for Sokoto today?`

**Say:**

"This is AMIN, the Africhain Market Intelligence Network. Traders can compare prices before restocking, and they can also report prices they see in their own market."

Optional command:

`Tomato na six thousand five hundred for Mile 12 today`

Then open `/market` to show the report.

---

## 2:05 - 2:30: Create A Receipt By Voice

**Action:** Open `/receipts`.

Click the receipt voice button and say:

`Customer Mr Chidi. Note thank you for buying from Mama Ada Provision Store.`

Then click `Download PDF receipt`.

**Say:**

"Because the sale is already recorded, Africhain can create a clean PDF receipt. The trader can even fill the receipt details by voice."

---

## 2:30 - 2:55: Close Strong

**Show:** Business dashboard.

**Say:**

"Africhain starts with a simple promise: talk to your business and it updates itself. But the data becomes much bigger. It helps traders recover debts, restock smarter, prove business activity, and eventually access credit, insurance, and better supply deals."

---

## Final Line

**Say:**

"Africhain turns everyday trader speech into business intelligence. That is how we bring informal businesses into the digital economy without asking them to change how they work."

---

## Commands To Memorize

1. `I sell six paint of rice for nine thousand naira`
2. `Mama Nkechi collect two bag of sugar, she never pay, na five thousand`
3. `Who dey owe me?`
4. `I buy ten bag of sugar, add am to stock`
5. `How much yam dey for Sokoto today?`
6. `Tomato na six thousand five hundred for Mile 12 today`
7. `Customer Mr Chidi. Note thank you for buying from Mama Ada Provision Store.`

---

## If Voice Fails

Say this:

"Markets are noisy, so Africhain also supports typed fallback. The same AI engine handles the command either way."

Then type the command and continue confidently.
