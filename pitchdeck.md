# Africhain Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: Africhain

**The voice-first operating system for Africa's informal traders.**

Africhain lets a trader speak naturally, then updates sales, debts, inventory, receipts, and market intelligence.

---

## Slide 2: Problem

Informal traders run serious businesses, but their records are often scattered across memory, paper, WhatsApp, and voice notes.

- Sales get forgotten.
- Customer debts are hard to track.
- Stock levels are unclear.
- Restocking decisions happen without reliable price data.
- Traders lack clean records for credit, insurance, and growth.

Existing tools feel too formal, too slow, and too far from real market behavior.

---

## Slide 3: Insight

Traders do not need to learn a new workflow first.

They already know how to run their business.

The product should meet them where they are: **speech**.

If everyday trader speech can become structured business data, then millions of informal businesses can become digitally visible without changing how they work.

---

## Slide 4: Solution

Africhain turns voice into business action through three clear modes:

1. **Log sales** - sales, debts, expenses, repayments, daily summary.
2. **Ask live prices** - market lookup and trader-reported prices through AMIN.
3. **Update inventory** - stock in, stock out, low-stock checks.

The trader speaks. Africhain understands, updates the records, and speaks back.

---

## Slide 5: Product Demo

In the demo, one trader can:

- Record a sale by voice.
- Add a customer debt.
- Ask who owes money.
- Restock inventory by voice.
- Compare live market prices.
- Generate a PDF receipt.
- View a dashboard and AI coach.

Every command changes real business data.

---

## Slide 6: AMIN Market Intelligence

**AMIN means Africhain Market Intelligence Network.**

Traders can ask:

- "How much yam dey for Sokoto today?"
- "Where rice cheap today?"
- "Tomato na six thousand five hundred for Mile 12 today."

AMIN compares prices, accepts trader reports, and turns local market activity into useful intelligence.

This moves Africhain beyond bookkeeping into commerce infrastructure.

---

## Slide 7: Inventory And Receipts

Africhain keeps inventory alive as the trader talks.

- Sales reduce stock.
- Restocking increases stock.
- Low-stock items are highlighted.
- The dashboard shows a clean inventory table.

Africhain also creates PDF receipts from recorded sales, and the receipt page can be filled by voice.

---

Africhain is built for the rhythm of the market.

---

## Slide 9: Business Model

Africhain can grow in layers:

- **Freemium app:** free basic records, paid advanced reports and receipts.
- **Market intelligence:** price trends, alerts, and buying recommendations.
- **Embedded finance:** use clean records for credit, insurance, and supplier access.
- **Group buying:** help traders buy together when AMIN finds price opportunities.

The more traders use Africhain, the stronger the data network becomes.
